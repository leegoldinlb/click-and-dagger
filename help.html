SECTOR HEIGHT (2D map, grid tools)

Dial a FLOOR and/or CEILING height, then RAISE/LOWER to brush cells or FILL to flood a whole room. Eye sits 0.5 above the floor; walls block steps taller than 0.5. Right-click with a height tool reverts a cell to its material default.

TEXTURES (2D map, grid tools)

Pick a TARGET (floor/wall or ceiling), click a texture, then paint cells to retexture them — independent of height and material. Right-click reverts a cell's texture. A cyan-bordered corner marks a custom ceiling.

DRAW MODE (vector sectors)

This is a vector map. Click (or Space) drops wall vertices; close the loop on the first point to make a sector.
